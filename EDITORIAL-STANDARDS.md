# Editorial Standards

1. Publish original material that answers a real reader question.
2. Verify factual claims and distinguish documented facts from opinion.
3. Disclose commercial relationships where applicable.
4. Use external links only when they are contextually relevant and accurately described.
5. Do not create pages solely to manipulate search rankings.
6. Do not fabricate authors, reviews, credentials, metrics, or test results.
7. Review material periodically and correct outdated information.
8. Respect copyright, privacy, and applicable law.

Accessibility Fundamentals for Video Experiences

An accessible video experience supports different devices, input methods, and access needs.

Captions

Captions should be synchronized and accurate, including meaningful sound information where appropriate. Check speaker identification and timing.

Keyboard operation

Play, pause, volume, captions, and full-screen controls should be reachable and operable without a mouse. Focus should remain visible and predictable.

Readable presentation

Use sufficient contrast, readable text, and controls that remain identifiable at different screen sizes. Do not communicate essential information through color alone.

Responsive layouts

Test common mobile widths and orientation changes. Controls should have usable touch targets and should not obscure important information.

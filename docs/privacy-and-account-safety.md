# Privacy and Account Safety

Read a service's privacy notice before registering. Understand what information is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained, and how account deletion works.

Use a unique password for each service, preferably stored in a reputable password manager. Enable multi-factor authentication when available. Never share recovery codes or one-time passwords.

Review browser permissions for notifications, camera, microphone, and location. Avoid unsolicited downloads and fake player updates. Sign out on shared devices and review active sessions when supported.

Consult the provider's own documentation for service-specific practices. Related resources are available from [Qavonia](https://qavonia.com/).

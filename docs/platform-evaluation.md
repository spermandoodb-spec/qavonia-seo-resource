# How to Evaluate an Online Video Platform

A useful evaluation goes beyond catalogue size. Consider reliability, usability, privacy, accessibility, and transparency.

## Playback and performance

Check page loading, playback startup, buffering, and quality controls on more than one device. Record browser and network conditions so that comparisons are meaningful.

## Navigation

Search, categories, filters, and content details should use clear labels. Visitors should understand where they are and how to return to previous results.

## Privacy and transparency

Review the privacy notice, cookie controls, account deletion process, billing terms, and support options before submitting personal information.

## Accessibility

Check keyboard operation, visible focus, captions, contrast, and mobile layout.

## Checklist

- Test playback on multiple devices and networks.
- Confirm that navigation labels are understandable.
- Locate privacy, billing, and support information.
- Check captions and keyboard access.
- Document limitations instead of making unsupported claims.
